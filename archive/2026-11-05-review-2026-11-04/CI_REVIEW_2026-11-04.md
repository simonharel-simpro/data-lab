### Cross-cutting finding — gitleaks is a no-op in CI (⚠️ drift, all trees)
The `gitleaks` hook (v8.30.1) runs `gitleaks git --pre-commit --redact --staged`. In CI,
`uvx pre-commit run --all-files` runs it on a fresh checkout that has **nothing staged**, so it scans nothing.
Secrets are therefore enforced **locally only**. Fix: add a dedicated `gitleaks detect --source . --log-opts=HEAD`
(or `gitleaks/gitleaks-action`) step to `ci.yml`, as in `recipes/ci/python-fastapi/github-ci.yml`.
